# Events and Meetings Specification

## Event workspace

One event record becomes the source of truth for:
- title and theme
- dates and venue
- committee
- tasks
- proposal
- budget and realization
- letters
- attendance
- documentation
- certificates
- final report

## Committee builder

- roles and members
- responsibilities
- drag ordering
- conflict warning
- export to SK attachment

## Budget

- categories
- planned amount
- realization
- attachment receipt
- approval status
- totals and variance
- no accounting claims beyond simple activity budget tracking

## Certificate batch

- template
- recipient list
- serial/document ID
- QR verification
- preview sample before generation
- ZIP export

## Meeting tracker

- agenda and invitation
- participant list and attendance
- notes
- decisions
- owner/PIC
- due date
- evidence and status

A decision remains visible on responsible users' dashboard until completed or cancelled with reason.
