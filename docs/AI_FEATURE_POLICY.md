# Optional AI Feature Policy

AI may assist with lesson-plan drafts, question drafts, feedback wording, and anonymized summaries only after core milestones.

## Required design

- Provider-independent interface.
- Server-only provider credentials.
- Per-user usage limits.
- Redaction of names, local codes, school identifiers, and free-text personal details.
- Prompt and output version metadata without storing sensitive prompt bodies by default.
- Teacher must review and explicitly save.
- Clear “AI draft” label.

## Forbidden

- final grade decisions;
- disciplinary decisions;
- student risk scoring;
- sending identifiable student records to a model;
- hidden AI use;
- promising unlimited free AI.
