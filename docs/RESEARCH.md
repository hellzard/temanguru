## UI inspiration patterns

- Today-focused quick actions.
- Calm sidebar on desktop and bottom navigation on mobile.
- Status cards that lead to a decision, not decorative metrics.
- Schedule, pending work, and recent activity grouped by urgency.

Visual references are inspiration only; do not copy layouts or assets.
