# Browser QA

Test:
- 320×568, 390×844, 768×1024, 1440×900;
- keyboard-only and visible focus;
- long Indonesian content and zoom/reflow;
- loading, empty, error, permission, and offline states;
- dialogs, bottom sheets, menus, tables, forms;
- touch targets and a non-drag alternative.

Capture evidence and retest after fixes.
